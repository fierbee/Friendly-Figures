  • About Us

The world we live in is finite. Many hold the value of having fun while it lasts, but often then not there are still costs. Many companies produce toys that aren't ecofriendly, and consumers purchase them believing that the environment should come second to their own enjoyment.

Here at Friendly Figures: we disagree. This world is finite, and it is one we all share, so no one person should have the right to sacrifice the wellbeing of others for their own gain; in this case we shouldn't let the environment fall by the wayside just for high edge toys

We believe that the solution to this problem isn't to remove toys and anything that poses a threat to the environment, since without joy, what is life? The best way to mediate this is by fulfilling both standards at the same time: making toys that provide enjoyment without sacrificing sustainability.

This led to the creation of Friendly Figures.

How We Are Sustainable

Here at friendly Figures, we use the newest technology to create high quality and sustainable action figures.

Friendly Figures are manufactured from environmentally friendly materials to reduce pollution—a mixture of recycled plastic, and recyclable plastics to ensure that your figurine collection is meant to last, while being easy to dispose of safely.

If you're planning to throw out your Friendly Figure, you can also donate it to a Friendly Figure store, where we'll recycle the figure and turn it anew.
